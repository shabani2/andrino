Naleta Story

A media direction rooted in my interest in storytelling and the need for richer narratives about Goma and the Great Lakes region.

Through Naleta Story, I want to explore people, ideas, businesses, culture and initiatives that reveal the many dimensions of life in our communities. The ambition is to create space for stories that inform, challenge assumptions and help audiences discover perspectives they might otherwise miss.
