01 / PROLOGUE

Stories, perspectives and possibilities.

Reframing narratives from the heart of the Great Lakes region through rigor, narrative balance, and contextual resonance.

Operating at the intersection of strategic communications, grassroots media, and creative development, my practice interrogates how human experience is chronicled across East DRC and the broader African continent. Rather than reducing complex social realities to predictable emergency bulletins, I document systemic resilience, cultural vitality, and ground-level innovation.

From institutional communications campaigns for international non-profits to independent investigative dispatches along Lake Kivu, my mandate remains constant: bridging the chasm between raw local realities and global audiences without forfeiting agency, subtlety, or lyrical integrity.

DISPATCH FROM GOMA // NORTH KIVU

Beyond the Headlines.

Africa is more than the stories most frequently told about it. Goma is more than the headlines that define it.

The mainstream lens captures Eastern DRC exclusively through the vocabulary of crisis, emergency, and depletion. While those hardships are real and lived, they do not exhaust the truth of our cities.

Between the shores of Lake Kivu and the shadows of the volcanic range lies a restless generator of visual art, entrepreneurial grit, musical movements, and dignified intellectual renewal. My work exists to document this broader, deeper, indelible reality.
