My Journey & Field Philosophy

From grassroots journalism in Kivu to strategic communications for international agency missions.

02.1 / TRAJECTORY

My professional journey has brought me into humanitarian communication, digital media, editorial storytelling and community-focused initiatives. I work across communication planning, content creation, social media, photography, video, reporting and the development of stories that help audiences understand people and projects beyond statistics.

Fundamental Inquiry Goma Dispatches

Growing up and working in the Great Lakes region has made me attentive to the complexity of places that are often described through a narrow lens. In Goma, conflict and uncertainty are part of the reality, but so are creativity, entrepreneurship, humour, culture, solidarity and ambition.

For me, communication is not simply about making information visible. It is about finding the right words, images and perspectives to make people care, understand and engage.

Guiding Principles

What I believe in.

Five structural convictions that shape every article, campaign, and documentary commission I undertake.

01 history_edu

Stories matter.

They define how individuals, communities, and whole regions are remembered. A carefully told narrative holds the power to reconstruct dignity and challenge ingrained indifference.

Core Tenet · Dignity
02 layers

Context matters.

Data and headlines without history or social realities fail to convey the truth. To communicate responsibly, we must take the time to untangle underlying causes, roots, and nuances.

Core Tenet · Complexity
03 groups

Local knowledge matters.

The people living the reality are always the best narrators of their experience. Effective communications must amplify local voices rather than speak over or displace them.

Core Tenet · Sovereignty
04 lightbulb

Creativity creates possibilities.

Form should elevate substance. Whether producing an investigative documentary film, a donor report, or a multimedia dispatch, thoughtful visual craft breaks fatigue and inspires meaningful action.

Core Tenet · Innovation
05 auto_stories

Learning never stops.

Media landscapes change, tools evolve, and humanity continuously shifts. Curiosity, self-critique, and rigorous listening are mandatory requirements for any impactful communicator.

Core Tenet · Humility
Horizon & Visions

What I am working towards.

I am focused on building independent editorial platforms, supporting culturally and socially meaningful initiatives across Central and Eastern Africa, and remaining stubbornly curious about how storytelling can foster genuine solidarity.

Whether collaborating with humanitarian agencies, cultural archives, or independent media publishers, the objective remains constant: rigorous, compassionate representation that endures.
